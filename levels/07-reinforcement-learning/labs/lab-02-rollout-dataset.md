# Lab 2: Rollout Dataset

## Objective

Generate and filter rollout data from StrongBench Finance Operations Simulator.

## Deliverable

Submit:

- rollout schema
- at least 100 rollouts
- reward distribution
- verifier component distribution
- filtered rows
- quality notes

## Checks

The lab passes if rollouts include actions, observations, verifier outputs, rewards, final state, and termination reason.
